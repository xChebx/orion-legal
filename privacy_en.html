ORION: New Dawn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game ORION: New Dawn (the “Game”) processes and how it is used. Developer: Kirill Rogov (“we”).

In short

  • The single-player game runs on your device and needs no network.
  • Ghost duels run through Apple Game Center. The Game does not send player data to the developer’s servers.
  • No sign-up. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number or address.
  • The Game has no chat and no free-text input that other players would see. Duels show the alias from your Game Center profile.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Single-player progress is stored on the device; we never receive it.

What is processed

  • Game progress and settings. Saves, achievements, language, graphics and audio preferences. Stored locally on the device. With iCloud Backup enabled these files may be included in your Apple backup according to your iOS settings — we have no access to it.
  • In-app purchases. Handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive only the fact and status of a purchase. Card details are never sent to us and are not available to us.
  • Game Center (Apple). If you are signed in, achievements and scores sync with your Apple account. For Ghost Duel, Apple also stores a compact gameplay progress record and match result linked to the participants. The Game receives an opponent's Game Center alias and profile image for display; the image is cached in memory only. The record contains no chat, e-mail, Apple ID or photograph.
  • Diagnostics. Anonymised crash and performance data provided by Apple if you allowed it in iOS settings.
  • Time check. At launch and when you return to the Game, it may send an anonymous request to apple.com to read the current time for in-game timers. The request carries no gameplay data or account information and does not go to our servers.

Why

  • to save and restore your progress;
  • to make purchases and purchase restoration work;
  • to send, receive and complete asynchronous Ghost Duels through Game Center;
  • to find and fix crashes and performance drops;
  • to keep in-game timers accurate (time check).

Third parties

  • Apple — App Store, purchases, Game Center, diagnostics, time check: Apple Privacy Policy.

Children and age

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Local saves are removed when you delete the Game. Ghost Duel matches are stored by Apple in Game Center; a completed match can be removed from the duel list, and Apple provides account controls. We do not operate server-side storage of player data. For access, correction or deletion requests, write to the address below — we reply within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest change is shown at the top of this page.
