ORION: New Dawn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game ORION: New Dawn (the “Game”) processes and how it is used. Developer: Kirill Rogov (“we”).

In short

  • The Game runs entirely on your device and does not contact any server.
  • No sign-up. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number or address.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics services, no ad networks and no servers of our own.
  • Game progress is stored on the device; we never receive it.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Everything listed below is handled either locally on the device or by Apple itself as part of the App Store.

What is processed

  • Game progress and settings. Saves, achievements, language, graphics and audio preferences. Stored locally on the device. With iCloud Backup enabled these files may be included in your Apple backup according to your iOS settings — we have no access to it.
  • In-app purchases. Handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive only the fact and status of a purchase. Card details are never sent to us and are not available to us.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in, achievements and scores sync with your Apple account. Apple controls the scope of that data.
  • Diagnostics. Anonymised crash and performance data provided by Apple if you allowed it in iOS settings.

Why

  • to save and restore your progress;
  • to make purchases and purchase restoration work;
  • to find and fix crashes and performance drops.

Third parties

  • Apple — App Store, purchases, Game Center, diagnostics: Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because there are no accounts, all game data is removed when you delete the Game from your device. For access, correction or deletion requests, write to the address below — we reply within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest change is shown at the top of this page.
